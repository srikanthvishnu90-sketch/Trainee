  - database: the trigger `career_apply_handoff_claim_guard` leaves such a row queued, untouched, for every claim except
    `career_apply_vm_claim_handoff` (the plain claim's rows plus the hand-off rows, each with `handoff {done_at, yes}` in the same

  - worker (`apply-vm.cjs`): no second call for the state. A row from the hand-off claim whose state is unreadable is let go unsent
    (`HANDOFF_STATE_UNREADABLE`; the sweep puts it back twice, then closes it not sent). A database without the hand-off claim, or
    a failing call, is asked with the plain claim.
  - runner (`parallel-apply.cjs`): "the employer's own page is never the form" is asked again after the Apply control is opened
    and on the page as it is before Submit (`followedPageRefusal`).
  - chat: the hand-off turn is held as any card's yes is (after "don't apply"), and does not run on a turn the Outlook connector
    or crisis text answers.
  - rollback: closes queued hand-off rows and held ones with no click recorded, unsent; re-runnable. The check is added NOT VALID
    and validated.
  - tests: `tests/architect/handoff-failclosed-20261113.test.ts` (Postgres, every migration), `tests/career-apply-vm-handoff-failclosed.test.cjs`.
- Chat (`supabase/functions/_shared/career_apply_handoff.ts`, wired in `handler-career.ts` before the card turn): "done" re-checks;
  "done" again shows the exact-answers card (its hash rides the reply's meta); a yes while that card is the thread's last word submits
  exactly that card; any other yes approves nothing. The VM (`apply-vm.cjs`) re-opens the posting in a new context, records the card,
  and presses Submit only on that yes while the page still holds exactly that card.
- **The person's own browser.** The person finishes the wall at the employer's page link in their own browser. Trainee's cloud runner
  then re-opens the posting in a NEW session: none of the person's cookies or sign-in travel. Where the employer clears the step on its

  marked `needs_extension` (the reason stays the wall's), the person is told to finish in their browser (the link) or with the Trainee
  extension, and it counts as the hand-off stop, never as a pass and never as sent.
