### 1.2 What the evidence settles, and one rule that overrides it
- **One column, no tables, no text boxes, contact in the body**: E1, E2, E3, E4. All fifteen are single-column.
- **Margins 0.5–1 in, body 10–12 pt, one family, consistent formatting, reverse chronological**: E5, E6. Every template is inside those bounds.
- **One page for students and early career; two pages for experienced people**: E6, E7 for one page. E10 says two pages are acceptable or preferred for experienced people. E20 caps federal resumes at two pages.
- **Bold titles over bullets, clear headers, an overview at the top, white space**: E1.
- **Quantified results**: E9. This is a content matter: Trainee never invents a number (D-007). The templates give results room, nothing more.
- **Name size, R6 scoped (architect ruling 2026-11-08)**: 148's R6 rule used to cap every run at 12 pt, the name included. It now applies to body text and headings (10–12 pt). The name may be 10–18 pt: the first line of page one in a PDF, or the first paragraph's own style in a DOCX (its own commit, with `tests/architect/r10-r6-name-size-20261108.test.ts`). The templates set the name at 14–18 pt. A name too wide for the column steps down a point at a time, never under 14 pt, and wraps only if it still does not fit.

### Geometry and fit
- **x stops**: the left margin (name, contact, headings, entries, lines), the bullet glyph's x, and the bullet text's x. The bullet text x is one hanging indent for every bullet and every wrapped bullet line. The right margin is where every date ends.
- **Rhythm**: lead = round to the nearest 0.5 of (body × the template's ratio), and unit = lead / 2. The name advances ⌈1.25 × its size / unit⌉ units; a heading advances at least 2 units; a body line advances 2 units. Gaps (header, section, after heading, entry) are whole units.
- **Fit**: start at the template's body size with its gaps. Then take one unit off each gap (never under its floor). Then go down 0.5 pt at a time, never below 10 pt. Try one page first, then two. A two-page template takes one page only at its loosest step: it is never squeezed. A record that does not fit two pages at the tightest step is set on as many pages as it needs, and that is said.
- **Pages**: a paragraph never splits across pages, so there are no widows or orphans. A heading keeps with its next block, and with that block's first bullet when the block is an entry. An entry keeps with its next block.
- **Words**: each printed line is a source line's own text, or a split of it at its dates. Sections follow the template's order. The inline header joins the contact and links lines with " | ". Nothing is added (r10/2 checks this word for word).
- **Name**: 14–18 pt by template (T05, T07, T09, T12, T13 and T15: 14; T01–T04, T08, T10 and T14: 16; T06 and T11: 18).
- **Fonts**: the PDF uses the standard faces (Helvetica or Times, regular and bold), never embedded (R6). The DOCX names Arial or Times New Roman, which are metric-compatible, so one width table measures both. A character the standard faces cannot print (Ł, ś, CJK) is refused with a reason, never printed as "?".
- **DOCX**: the same paragraphs, with the same exact line heights, spacing before taken from the PDF's own baselines, the same hanging indents, and a right tab on the margin. Entries keep the date's room as a right indent, with the tab past it. Each page starts with a page break before the paragraph that opens it in the PDF. Paragraph borders give back their 1.625 pt.

## 3. The rater (career_resume_rater.ts)

Scores the person's own file from its measured geometry: pdf.js positions and widths for a PDF, resolved paragraph
properties for a DOCX. A criterion that cannot be measured is left out, and the total is rescaled to 100.

| criterion | points | hard | measured as |
|-----------|-------:|:----:|-------------|
| columns | 15 | yes | PDF: no line starting past 40% of the width except flush-right dates, and the file's own text order equal to top-to-bottom order. DOCX: one section column, no side-by-side table cells |
| headings | 8 | | standard headings found (at least two of experience, education, skills); non-standard caps headings named |
| type | 10 | | body size (character-weighted mode) 10–12 pt; nothing under 9 pt; common ATS-safe faces |
| margins | 8 | | left and top 0.5–1 in; right under 0.5 in fails (when no line reaches it, it is reported as "at least"); bottom at least 0.5 in |
| edges | 10 | | bullet glyph x, bullet text x, wrapped-line x and left edge: one value each, ±0.5 pt |
| dates | 8 | | all dates flush right to one x, ±0.5 pt, or all at one x; dates pushed right with spaces named |
| gaps | 7 | | same-level transitions (into an entry, into a heading) equal, ±0.5 pt |
| containers | 10 | yes | no tables, text boxes or images holding text; a PDF with under 40 words of real text is a scan |
| length | 6 | | pages within 1 below 5 years of dates, at most 2 above (a CV is exempt) |
| contact | 8 | yes | name, email and phone in the body's first lines; a header/footer-only contact is named |
| roundtrip | 10 | yes | PDF: pdf.js's text layer equals the operator walk, word for word, with no unreadable runs. DOCX: the paragraph walk equals 148's DOCX parser |

A hard criterion fails when it gets under half its points. **KEEP rule: score ≥ 85, no hard failure, and (for a DOCX) 148's own parse-check passes the file as it is** keeps their format
and changes only the content:
- a DOCX is the "own" path, with the tailored resume put into their file (review of #620; `planOwnEdits`, `verifyOwnPrinted` in `career_resume_design.ts`):
  - Each paragraph after the header is mapped to the person's confirmed record: a fact's title, one of its original bullets, or the skills line.
  - Within an entry, the tailored bullets take that entry's bullet paragraphs in order, reworded and re-ordered, each keeping the paragraph's own formatting.
  - The skills paragraph takes the tailored skills line.
  - Bullets and entries the tailoring dropped are removed, and a heading left with nothing under it goes too.
  - The plan is refused when anything cannot be placed exactly: a paragraph that is on no confirmed record, or a tailored line with no paragraph to go in.
  - After the file is made, its printed content lines must be exactly the tailored resume's audited lines, and its header must carry the profile's name and email.
  - The printed lines then pass the same materials guard as the tailored document.
  - If any of this fails, their layout is not used: the file is made in the recommended template and the reply says why.
  - The DOCX side of 148's R6 counts only the styles the document uses (its defaults, the styles its paragraphs and runs name, and their basedOn chains), so a real Word file's unused built-in sizes no longer fail it. The rater requires the same parse-check before it keeps a DOCX, so the two agree;
- a PDF is rebuilt from its measured geometry (`ownPdfSpec`) only when that rebuild is faithful: a Helvetica/Arial or Times face, body 10–12 pt, a name of 10–18 pt (R6 scoped), measurable margins and leading, and flush-right dates;
- otherwise their Word copy is used if there is one; otherwise the best of the fifteen is offered with the reason.

Under the rule, tailoring makes the file in the default layout and offers `recommendTemplate(role, level)` with a
plain-words why. "Keep mine" takes the keep path even under 85, never a PDF that cannot be rebuilt faithfully. "Use
template N" is taken as asked.

## 4. The pixel checker (career_resume_pixel.ts)

It reads each output PDF back with pdf.js, from the show-text operators, so a bullet glyph stays separate from its words.
It holds every line to the template's declared geometry and fails on:

| rule | fails when |
|------|------------|
| grid | a baseline is off the grid by more than 0.5 pt, or an item starts off the x stops by more than 0.5 pt |
| indent | bullets do not share one glyph x and one hanging text x, wrapped lines included |
| dates | a date is not flush on the right margin or not on its entry's baseline |
| gaps | two same-level transitions have different gaps |
| widow | a page opens with a wrapped line, or closes with a heading or an entry whose content starts the next page |
| overflow | text runs past any margin |
| docx_text | the DOCX's words are not the PDF's, in order |
| fonts | a face is outside the template's two, an embedded font appears, or a size is outside 10–12 pt (the name: its template's size, 10–18 pt) |

r10/4 mutates a clean file once per rule and proves each rule fires: 0.6 pt off-grid, a 1 pt moved glyph, a 0.6 pt moved
text, a date 1 pt short, one uneven gap, a page break inside a bullet, an overflow, a foreign face, a 9.5 pt run, and a DOCX
with other words.
