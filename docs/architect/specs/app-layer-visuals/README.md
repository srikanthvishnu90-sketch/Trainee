families (Screen 0, F-01 to F-15 and the negotiation variant, p41-86), the neutral document pages (shortlist,
application packet, research brief; p87-89) and the login, connection and output-state panels (p90-94). Every value
in them is fictional or a dummy; the owner's actual screenshots and resume (PDF pages 36-40) are NOT included.

structure, sections, order, states and visual hierarchy as closely as Trainee's brand (brand.ts) allows. The written
spec (../APP-LAYER-15-FIXES.md, section C) gives page numbers for each family. Builders compare their screenshots to
these pages side by side.
