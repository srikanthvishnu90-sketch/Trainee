New findings in this pass:
- **A heading can pass as a fact.** The fake draft first took an SEC filing's heading ("Form 10-K Hollow Oak Logistics Inc.") as a fact, because "10-K" looked like a figure, and the facts guard accepted it: the words are on the page. The fake draft now ignores form names, but the product's guard would accept the same fragment from a real model.
- **Fixture facts read as fixtures.** Facts converted from JSON keep their source's own words ("Silverpine Robotics (Wikidata Q99000101): ..."), which are accurate but stiff.
- **No reconciliation across sources.** Two sources may give different figures for one thing (a stub site's headcount and a 10-K's); the brief can cite both side by side without reconciling them.
